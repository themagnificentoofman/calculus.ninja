# Problem-bank authoring

Before changing or adding content, read `../../docs/bank/PROBLEM-AUTHORING-STANDARDS.md` and `../../docs/bank/NOTATION-AND-SOLUTIONS.md`. Follow their mathematical-decision, finite-domain, options-visible difficulty, independent-verification, notation, source-binding, lineage, provenance, and admission requirements. These apply equally to bulk families, standalone questions, and adaptations. Complete the authoring checklist and add evidence-backed practices that worked to the standing guide and release handoff; distinguish local checks from imported or deployed results.

Preserve frozen constructors used to reconstruct existing immutable releases. Do not update a certificate hash merely to make a changed constructor pass. A new instance or family needs a source-bound reviewed solution, strict rendering, and independently checked mathematics before it can be admitted. Run the publication gate through `admin.mjs` / the delta workflow. Unknown or unreviewed families must fail closed; never bypass the gate by setting a validation flag.

Preserve historical integrity while requiring active legacy content to earn its place through demonstrated quality and usefulness. Retention, repair, reclassification and withdrawal need evidence tied to the actual content; age or raw item count alone does not justify a decision. Useful older fluency remains eligible when it meets the same review standards as new work.

Read `../../docs/bank/problem-quality/LEGACY-POLICY.md` before admitting legacy content. Experimental opt-in defaults off; it is not an exception to quality admission. Exact source/review evidence, original construction lineage and the Legacy marker are required. Default practice horizons must pass without optional legacy; competitive modes use the common nonlegacy pool.

Never rewrite published hashes, answer keys, saved games, ratings, or replay snapshots to fix explanation formatting. Use reviewed presentation corrections for non-semantic clarifications, and new immutable revisions for mathematical changes.
- Compare unordered choice sets and keyed values across constructions and separately retained lineages. Check whether one numerical property, a relation among the four choices, or two easy bounds determines the key. Review those clues independently from the historical teaching and replay answers that survive a choice repair.

For choice reviews, test relationships among all displayed choices across related constructions. Record option-only predictions and misses, including reciprocal pairs and clues combining magnitude with answer form. Recheck the catalog after repairs. When a revision is held because a lesson or a saved solution supplies its task, bind the hold across grading contracts for that exact revision. Test the operator, runtime and stored playback separately.

Qualify a returning mixed session with the regular capacity horizon on every start and retained displayed identities. Record the first exhausted request. A run with a decreasing horizon is a separate diagnostic.

When a proposed choice set contains zero, one, positive infinity and an integer greater than one, test whether the integer’s parity predicts the keyed limit across every matching family. Run `node scripts/bank/quality/limit-menu-audit.mjs SERVED.jsonl NEW-REPORT.json` on the complete candidate catalog. Inspect the recorded matches and misses, including related topics. A known answer-bearing solution remains relevant after the options change.

Keep a superseded assessment revision out of new admissions when its easiest-route classification, worked mathematics or stated alternative technique needed correction. Verify the earlier saved grade separately. An exact mathematical match on the final value alone does not clear incorrect geometry in a worked step.

When only the choices change, reuse a first solution only after matching the exact displayed statement, assumptions and dependencies of that proof. Seal each replacement menu before exposing its key to the options reviewer. Check all six pairwise differences, evaluate the easiest route with the choices visible, then rescreen the final membership alongside retained historical questions. Preserve earlier failures and change the destination revision whenever its issued content changes.

A roster cap can leave an independently reviewed source out of active free-response practice. To reuse it in another format, verify its exact earlier admission, current registry, recorded omission reason and current holds. Reject missing, stale or conflicting evidence. Join masked options to answer records with equal counts and exact per-row hashes. An unmatched pair is an error and must remain visible in the review.

A discovered answer-rank clue calls for a catalog-wide sibling check at the actual served tier. Compare rational choices exactly. For nonrational choices use proved disjoint intervals, retain unresolved comparisons, and independently verify the source keys and easiest valid method. Test an observed relation on separate siblings and across option order. For Study transfers, compare the literal substitution and the learner-visible answer. Retain per-item reasons and suspended source revisions before recompiling affected reserves.

When a later reviewed MC revision depends on an earlier private revision absent from the physical parent, bind the exact earlier source snapshot, its approval, its original row bytes and its successor. Preserve missing predecessors as retired immutable rows with zero pool slots. Include them in the real import, decoded-page checks and cumulative budget. An ambiguous earlier identity needs separate historical resolution before it can fill a required predecessor slot.

To carry a screenshot measurement to a changed roster, recover its observed viewport samples, the exact per-row HTML digest, source closure, and the original packet. Verify the original packet hashes, ordered identities, viewport measurements, built assets, frame, fonts, browser binary and measurement script. Use a roster-to-original-receipt map for matching rows and measure the remaining ones at both widths. A reordered roster does not require recomputing the same isolated card measurements. Exercise the full routed screen separately for any changed surrounding interaction or layout.

For an additive bank rehearsal, identify the unchanged immutable subset by the complete admission key, record hash, selection hash and review receipt. The source image must match its recorded SHA and previous guarded comparison in both directions for every physical table. Bind the previous successful idempotency check by its receipt. Reuse those staging results, prepare and retry each new row through the native writer, then run the full destination guard, export, rollback and disabled-operator checks. A failed process before the guarded writes provides no destination receipt.

Keep solved assessment dossiers in private release evidence. Before preparing public source, verify the complete original archive, its content and reviewer digests, and its exact renderer mapping. A public CI job can verify a frozen mapping from its digest and strict field schema. Every new admission and private release qualification still requires the full dossier and independent mathematical checks. Test a changed public mapping, absent evidence and a wrong archive. Scan the public source and emitted browser assets separately for answer keys, worked answers and private files.

When a family always produces a square integer, test whether choosing the largest positive square predicts its keys. Run `node scripts/bank/quality/square-menu-audit.mjs SERVED.jsonl NEW-REPORT.json` on the exact served catalog and inspect matches, misses and abstentions by family. Review short mathematical routes even if the repaired menu defeats this rule. A correct prediction on one item alone does not establish a family defect.
