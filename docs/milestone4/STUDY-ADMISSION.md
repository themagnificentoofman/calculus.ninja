## Reviewing a changed practice roster

Build the exposure inventory from the actual eligible catalog in both Legacy scopes. Bind the sorted problem IDs, revisions, content hashes, formats, normalized constructions and the delivered choices to the exact release and selection source. A converted multiple-choice question also retains the hash of its actual free-response source, including when the source belonged to an earlier reviewed roster.

For a successor, retain the previous inventory and compare the actual literal teaching units, search projections, scored statements, complete choice menus and worked solutions. Reuse an individual comparison only when both sides and its mathematical scope are unchanged. Review every addition or revision against all relevant retained teaching, and each new teaching unit against the full destination. Recheck sibling or menu-wide clues where their population changed. Record removals separately and preserve the original finding and replay history.

Record the mathematical operation that remains after exposure. A lecture can name a method or prove a related theorem while a scored case still requires a branch, integral or extremal argument. If a worked family gives the answer for the supplied parameters, document the exact substitution or change of units and check the resulting scored answer. Path labels and readiness prompts need their own comparison because a title can reveal a conclusion before a lesson opens. A path enters the public projection only when its constituent lessons have individually passed their checks.

A complete answer may be distributed across two lessons. One worked example can supply the normalized direction and another can evaluate both gradient components against that same direction. If the graded question gives the gradient and changes only its numerical entries, record the remaining component arithmetic and compare it with the worked operation. If the gradient must be derived from a new field, record that extra differentiation separately. Check every finite source row before extending either decision to siblings.

Check each displayed statement even when the construction name is familiar. A family may contain both a two-dimensional rectangle and a three-dimensional box. In a worked solution, an independently offered shortcut can give an entire assessed operation even when the main integral uses a different method. Compare coordinate dimension, supplied derivatives, fixed directions and normalization against every relevant worked route. Bind the selected source revision and its served revision separately when converting a question to multiple choice.

## Scored practice links

Each scored exercise link binds the lesson and exercise revision, curriculum topic and tier, and reviewed technique. The reader offers one-question multiple-choice and free-response drills only for formats that passed the regular selector with the complete reserve. Broader topic practice has a separate link. At round start the server applies the account’s current history, exact filter and active catalog.

To produce a candidate projection, run the following command against a coherent local snapshot containing the active guarded catalog and its matching source and admission registry. ```
